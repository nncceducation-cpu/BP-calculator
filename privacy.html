Privacy Policy

Neonatal Blood Pressure Centile Calculator

Privacy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

The Neonatal Blood Pressure Centile Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information, patient information, device identifiers, or usage data.

Gestational age, day of life, and blood pressure values are processed locally on the user's device. The app does not create user accounts and does not use advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, cookies, location services, camera, microphone, contacts, or external health-data services.

The app provides educational reference calculations. Users must not enter names, medical record numbers, dates of birth, or other identifying information.

External links

The evidence section may identify published medical references. If a user independently opens an external website, that website's privacy practices apply.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be directed to the Calgary Neonatal Neuro-Critical Care Program through the developer contact shown in the applicable app store listing.
