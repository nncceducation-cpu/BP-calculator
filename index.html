How to interpret the result

Reference, not a treatment threshold

These centiles describe a population distribution. They do not define adequate organ perfusion and have not been validated as treatment triggers.

Measurement method matters

Use a correctly sized cuff on a settled infant. Oscillometric SBP can exceed intra-arterial SBP, especially at low pressures. MAP generally shows the closest agreement.

Evidence sources and calculator method

The calculator reproduces the 50th, 95th, and 99th percentile table compiled by Dionne, Abitbol, and Flynn for postmenstrual ages 26 to 44 weeks. Values between the published 2-week PMA rows use linear interpolation. No extrapolation is performed outside 26 to 44 weeks.

Recent first-week studies confirm strong effects of gestational age and postnatal age, but their centile models are method-specific. The early-age warning prevents the post-transitional table from being mistaken for a first-week nomogram.

  2. van Zadelhoff AC, et al. Age-dependent changes in arterial blood pressure in neonates during the first week of life. Br J Anaesth. 2023;130:585-594.
  3. Kiss JK, et al. Oscillometric arterial blood pressure in haemodynamically stable neonates in the first 2 weeks of life. Pediatr Nephrol. 2023;38:3369-3378.
  4. Hillman NH, Williams HL, Petersen RY. Oscillatory blood pressure values in newborn infants. Neonatology. 2025;122:138-145.
  5. Dionne JM, et al. Method of blood pressure measurement in neonates and infants. J Pediatr. 2020;221:23-31.e5.
